A few honest answers.

Does it need macOS 26? +

Yes. Orate is built on SpeechAnalyzer, the on device speech framework macOS 26 ships. That is the entire reason there is nothing to download: the model is already on your Mac and Apple keeps it current. There is no fallback for older systems, because shipping one would mean bundling a model, which is the thing Orate exists to avoid.

Does my voice leave my Mac? +

No. Recognition runs on the Neural Engine. There is no account, no server, and nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing to send.

Is this a subscription? +

No. One payment, and it is yours. Every incumbent worth comparing against is a subscription for software that runs entirely on your own machine.

What happens without the Accessibility permission? +

Orate still works. Without it, dictated text goes to your clipboard instead of being typed at your cursor, and you paste it yourself. Refusing that permission does not break the app, it just makes it one step slower.

Does it rewrite what I said? +

Not by default. Orate types the words you said. An optional cleanup pass removes filler words and fixes punctuation, off unless you turn it on, and it never changes your meaning.
